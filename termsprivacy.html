Privacy Policy

This service uses the Youtube API to identify videos inside a given playlist that are blocked or unavailable in a given country. You can use this service without logging in, in which case no information is collected from you. You can only look at public or unlisted playlists, and you cannot remove videos from your playlists through the service.
Alternatively, you can log in to your Youtube account. When this happens, the service obtains a list of all your playlists. This is to see whether you own a playlist and are therefore allowed to remove videos from it. If you are logged in, you can use the service to look at your private playlists, and you can use the service to remove blocked or unavailable videos from any of your playlists.
This service does not collect or save any of your personal information, and only uses your login for the above purposes. Your login is not saved anywhere, and is only valid for a single page visit. As such, you will need to log in each time you use this service. Google however will remember that you have allowed this service to access your Youtube account, and you can revoke this access at any time on the Google security settings page.
You can see exactly what this site is doing by looking directly at the code. If you have any further questions about this service, you may contact me at dcragusa at gmail dot com.
